# Operations Research 📂

### 📖 Course Overview
Mathematical and algorithmic optimization for real-world business and engineering applications:
- **Variable Domain Approaches**: Continuous, discrete, and mixed-integer optimization, with special emphasis on discrete (combinatorial) optimization.
- **Information Availability**: Deterministic, stochastic, and robust optimization frameworks.
- **Large-Scale Optimization**: Delayed column/row generation and decomposition methods (Dantzig-Wolfe, Benders decomposition).
- **Core Methods & Topics**: Linear programming (Simplex algorithm, duality, dual simplex, interior-point methods), Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP solving strategies and formulations), combinatorial optimization, network planning and scheduling (max flow, min cut, vehicle routing, knapsack problems), constraint satisfaction and constraint programming (backtracking and local search heuristics), decision trees (decision theory), stochastic programming, Markov decision processes (MDP) and stochastic dynamic programming, and robust linear optimization under polyhedral uncertainty.
- **Applications**: Machine learning optimization, business process improvement, project scheduling, timetabling, logistics, and financial mathematics.
