# Quantum Computing 📂

### 📖 Course Overview
An introduction to quantum mechanics with an emphasis on essential concepts from linear algebra and Dirac bra-ket notation. The distinction between classical bits and quantum bits (qubits), including the geometric representation of qubit states on the Bloch sphere. Quantum cryptography and secure communications: the BB84 quantum key distribution (QKD) protocol. Quantum entanglement and non-local correlations. Unitarity in quantum mechanics and the time evolution of closed quantum states. Quantum logic gates and core quantum algorithms: Deutsch's algorithm, Shor's factoring algorithm, and Grover's database search algorithm. Overview of candidate technologies for physical quantum hardware implementation: photon polarization, Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR), trapped ions, neutral atoms, superconducting Josephson junctions, and semiconductor quantum dots.
