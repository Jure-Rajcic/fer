# Fundamentals of Plasma Physics 📂

### 📖 Course Overview
A historical overview of plasma physics, fundamental definitions, and characteristic plasma parameters. Debye shielding and quasi-neutrality. Plasma oscillation frequency and cyclotron frequency. Collision processes in ionized gases and collision frequency. Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD): foundational equations, plasma equilibria, and macroscopic fluid approximations. Plasma system instabilities. Waves in cold and warm plasmas. Plasma diagnostics: temperature, density, and magnetic field measurement techniques (Langmuir probes, spectroscopic methods, interferometry). Types of plasmas and practical applications: artificially produced laboratory and industrial plasmas, fusion confinement concepts (tokamaks, stellarators), atmospheric plasmas, and space/astrophysical plasmas.
