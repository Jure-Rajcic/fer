# Artificial Intelligence Ethics 📂

### 📖 Course Overview
*Etika umjetne inteligencije* — Ethical, philosophical, and legal frameworks governing modern artificial intelligence and machine learning systems. Topics include algorithmic fairness, bias mitigation, transparency, explainability (XAI), privacy, accountability, and the societal impact of autonomous systems.
